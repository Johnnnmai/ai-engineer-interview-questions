    Questions

    How does the plan decide what to study each day? It counts the days from today to your interview, keeps the last two days for a mock round and a light warm-up, and spreads the track's questions over the remaining days, Entry level first, then Mid, then Senior. With fewer days it shows fewer questions per day, up to four, starting with the most common ones.
    What happens if my interview is more than 60 days away? The plan covers the final 60 days and tells you the date to start. Before then, one question every few days and a weekly mock round is enough to keep the material fresh.
    What if my interview is today or the date has passed? If it is today you get a short warm-up: three core questions to re-read, one timed mock round and a self-check. If the date has passed, the page says so and asks for your next date instead of building a plan.
    Is there a dedicated data science or PM question bank? Not yet. The data science and PM tracks use the free AI engineer pages that overlap with those interviews, such as A/B testing, evaluation and LLM product design, plus daily practice tasks without links for SQL, statistics, product sense and behavioral rounds.
    What is the self-check link at the end of each day? It opens the free answer self-check with that day's question loaded. Answer out loud, paste or type what you said, and get a score against the key points in about 30 seconds.
    Can I print the plan? Yes. Use the print button. The printed version keeps the days, questions and checkboxes and drops the form and buy buttons.
