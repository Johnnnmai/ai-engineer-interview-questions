# Exponent (Now Aced) Alternatives for AI Engineer Interviews (2026)

**Meta description:** Exponent (now Aced) vs Hello Interview, ByteByteGo, Educative and free options for AI engineer interview prep: prices checked Nov 2, 2026.

Exponent now operates as Aced (its pricing page reads "Aced (formerly Exponent)"). It is a broad membership with courses, video answers, peer mock interviews and an AI Engineering track. If you mainly need AI engineer prep (RAG, agents, LLM system design, evaluation), the alternatives below range from a free question bank to one-time guides and other subscriptions. Prices were read from each seller's own page on Nov 2, 2026.

## What Aced (formerly Exponent) offers

- **Plans:** Basic is free (sample lessons, 5 peer mocks per month, sample questions, basic job referrals). Pro lists $79 per month, or $12 per month on the annual plan. Expert lists $99 per month, or $20 per month on the annual plan, and adds the AI coaching suite. Source: [aced.io/upgrade](https://www.aced.io/upgrade).
- **What Pro includes:** all courses, video and written solutions, company-specific guides, unlimited peer mocks, the full question database, priority job referrals and a Discord community.
- **AI Engineering track:** an overview plus courses on system design interviews with AI, GenAI interviews and concepts, practical coding and project interviews, coding interviews and behavioral interviews. Source: [Aced AI Engineering overview](https://www.aced.io/courses/intro-ai-engineering/intro-ai-engineering-overview).
- **Policies:** memberships renew automatically; the pricing page offers full refunds within 5 days of purchase.

Aced does a lot well: peer mock interviews, video answers and coverage of many roles beyond AI engineering. Choose an alternative if you want a one-time purchase, a narrower AI-only focus or a free starting point.

## How to choose

1. **You need live practice with people:** stay with Aced, whose peer mocks are its strongest feature. No PDF replaces a person asking follow-ups.
2. **Your loop is mostly system design:** compare Aced with Hello Interview, which centers on system design and AI mock interviews.
3. **Your loop is LLM-heavy and your budget is small:** start with the free [AI engineer question bank](https://aie.valenx.org/), the [timed mock round](https://aie.valenx.org/mock-interview/) and the [answer self-check](https://aie.valenx.org/answer-check/), then add a one-time guide for the gaps.
4. **You prefer to own the material:** a book or PDF is a single payment with no renewal to cancel.

## A free 2-week plan before you subscribe to anything

- **Days 1 to 3:** answer 20 questions from the [question bank](https://aie.valenx.org/questions/) out loud and score them with the self-check.
- **Days 4 to 7:** work through [RAG interview questions](https://aie.valenx.org/guides/rag-interview-questions/) and [AI agent interview questions](https://aie.valenx.org/guides/ai-agent-interview-questions/).
- **Days 8 to 12:** practice one prompt a day from [AI system design interview questions](https://aie.valenx.org/guides/ai-system-design-interview-questions/).
- **Days 13 to 14:** run full mock rounds and fix the weakest topic. Build your own dates with the [study plan](https://aie.valenx.org/study-plan/).
