About AI Engineer Interview Questions

AI Engineer Interview Questions (aie.valenx.org) is a free study site published by Valenx Press, the publisher of The 0→1 Interview Playbook Series. Our books and guides are written by a Valenx Press contracted author.

What this site covers

  • 100 common AI engineer interview questions with structured answers: LLM fundamentals, RAG, agents, evaluation, fine-tuning and production systems.
  • Free practice tools: an answer self-check, a study plan, an LLM cost calculator and a mock round.
  • Company and topic guides based on public interview reports.

How the content is written

Questions reflect common public interview patterns. Answers are written as frameworks you can adapt, not scripts to memorise. We are not affiliated with any company named on the site, and company names are used only to describe publicly reported interview processes.

Paid products

The site links to The 0→1 AI Engineer Interview Playbook and other Valenx Press guides. Buying is optional; every question page is free to read.
