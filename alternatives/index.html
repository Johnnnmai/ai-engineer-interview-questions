FAQ

What is the best alternative to the ByteByteGo ML System Design Interview book? If your loop is classic ML system design, the book is a strong pick. If your loop is LLM-heavy (RAG, agents, evals), look for material built around those topics, such as the free 100-question bank here or the 0 to 1 AI Engineer Interview Playbook.
Is Grokking the Machine Learning Interview worth it? If you already have an Educative subscription, it is a structured course you get as part of it. If you would buy the subscription only for this course, compare the cost with a one-time guide.
What is the difference between AI engineer and ML engineer interview prep? ML engineer loops lean on training, features and classic system design. AI engineer loops in 2026 lean on LLM applications: retrieval, agents, tool calling, evals, cost and latency.
Are free AI engineer interview questions enough? For screens, often yes, if you answer out loud and get feedback. Use the free bank, the mock round timer and the self-check first, then buy a guide only for the gaps you find.
Do any of these replace mock interviews with a person? No. They are reading and self-practice. A mock with a peer or mentor still catches things a page cannot.
